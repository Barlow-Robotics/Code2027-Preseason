Try it

Open the simulator and graph

  1. Save your files. Stop any running simulator.
  2. In VS Code, press Ctrl + Shift + P. Choose WPILib: Simulate Robot Code, then Sim GUI.
  3. Connect the Xbox controller. Drag it from System Joysticks onto Joystick[1].
  4. Open AdvantageScope (WPILib). Choose File → Connect to Simulator, then NetworkTables if asked.
  5. Add a Line Graph. Expand Telemetry → Elevator.
  6. Drag in Target_m, Actual_m, MovementActive, and Velocity_mps.
  7. In Sim GUI, select Teleoperated and Enabled.

Need help finding the controls? See the screenshots.

Plot selection and movement

Add SelectedHeight_m to your graph. Add a Table tab for SelectedLevel.

  1. Tap Y. Selection becomes MID. The elevator keeps moving after release.
  2. Wait. At arrival, MovementActive becomes false.
  3. Tap Y again. It goes to TOP. Another Y press stays at TOP.
  4. Tap A, wait, then tap A again. It goes MID → BOTTOM.
  5. Tap A at BOTTOM. Selection stays BOTTOM.

Target_m may end slightly away from SelectedHeight_m: cleanup holds the measured arrival position.

Screenshot placeholder: one BOTTOM → MID → TOP trip.

Interrupt a trip

  1. Tap Y, then disable before arrival.
  2. MovementActive becomes false. Without motor power, gravity may lower the elevator.
  3. Enable without pressing a button. It must not resume the old trip.

It may settle toward the captured hold position. SelectedLevel still remembers the old destination; the next increase or decrease starts from that selection.

Try two quick presses

Predict: what will two quick Y presses do?

  1. Return to BOTTOM and wait.
  2. Tap Y. Wait one second, then tap Y again before arrival.
  3. Read SelectedLevel. It stays MID.
  4. Wait for arrival. Tap Y again: now it selects TOP.

The binding reuses one command. Scheduling it again while it is running does nothing.
